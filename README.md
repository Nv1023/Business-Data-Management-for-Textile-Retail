# 🏪 Textile Business Analytics | Inventory & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Efficient inventory management is essential for improving profitability in retail businesses. This project analyzes six months of operational data from a textile shop to identify inventory inefficiencies, sales trends, purchasing patterns, and factors affecting profit.

Using business analytics techniques, the project provides data-driven recommendations that help optimize inventory levels, improve product availability, and enhance overall business performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The textile shop faced several operational challenges:

- Overstocking of slow-moving products
- Stock shortages of high-demand products
- Declining profit margins
- Difficulty monitoring inventory
- Lack of data-driven purchasing decisions
- High operating expenses affecting profitability

The objective of this project is to analyze business data and provide actionable insights to improve inventory management and increase profitability.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The analysis uses six months of real business data, including:

- Sales Data
- Purchase Data
- Inventory Records
- Business Expenses

The datasets were cleaned, validated, and organized for analysis before calculating business performance metrics.

## 💡 Key Findings

- Several products remained overstocked for extended periods.
- Fast-moving products occasionally experienced stock shortages.
- Inventory turnover varied significantly across product categories.
- Business expenses remained relatively stable while profits declined.
- A small group of products contributed significantly to overall sales.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

- Maintain optimal inventory levels based on demand.
- Increase focus on high-contributing products.
- Reduce overstocking of slow-moving inventory.
- Monitor inventory KPIs regularly.
- Improve purchasing decisions using historical sales data.
- Review operating expenses periodically to improve profit margins.
